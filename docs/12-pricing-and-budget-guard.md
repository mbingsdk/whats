# Pricing, confirmation and budget guard

## Evidence and policy boundary

Official public pricing and messaging policy were re-inspected on **2026-09-09**. Pricing describes delivered-message charging by market/category, free service messages and applicable utility replies, utility/authentication volume tiers and eligible 72-hour free entry. Messaging policy still uses legacy conversation-pricing wording. Executable billing must follow reviewed effective-dated pricing/rate evidence, not inherit that wording; policy still governs consent and messaging permission. Exact rates, eligibility/account details and future changes remain **NEEDS VERIFICATION**; no numeric Meta rates are included. [Sources and discrepancy](21-research-register.md#conflicts-that-affect-implementation).

Default: **block dispatch when potentially chargeable cost cannot be bounded by a verified policy**. This applies to every message type, including non-template messages; a service-window permission does not itself establish zero cost. A verified zero-cost policy can pass without paid confirmation, while consent, permissions and other guards still apply. A manual administrator checkbox cannot turn unknown pricing into known pricing. To allow uncertain discounts conservatively, use a verified non-discounted upper bound and explicit approval/confirmation, not an invented price.

Pricing engine input: org/app/WABA/phone, opaque recipient identity and verified market if available, payload/template version/category, proposed dispatch interval, current service/free-entry evidence, currency, account/rate variant, applicable volume tier facts, policy effective dates and usage evidence. An E.164 prefix is a rate-card market input when the official policy supports it, not a geolocation claim. Unknown/hidden phone or ambiguous market requires verified routing/pricing metadata or blocks.

Policy schema supports changing billable units (delivered message, future verified units), categories including SERVICE, effective dates, allowances, volume tiers, authentication-international variants, entry-point rules, rounding and official source provenance. Any allowance is reserved atomically like a budget; do not assume a future free-message quota without verification. Current contract/account discounts/provider fees/tax treatment require company confirmation and separate line items. The initial guard controls Meta exposure; total invoice guard needs explicit non-Meta fee policies.

## Preflight and authorization

Preflight is a dry run: permission, sender capability, window, content/template, eligibility, price, budgets and approval requirements. Return blocked reasons, evidence timestamps, estimate/upper bound, currency, estimate expiry and scope hash. It does not reserve money or guarantee later execution. Default estimate TTL 60 seconds for interactive sends; campaign estimates cover a reviewed interval and are still rechecked at dispatch.

Use an opaque random single-use pricing authorization stored by digest. Bind canonical scope hash to organization, actor/key, phone/WABA, recipient identifier namespace/value digest, conversation, exact rendered content/media hashes, template version/language/category, origin, quote/policy, max amount/currency, required management authority, validity interval and nonce. Server stores evidence, not client-supplied truth. No token in query string/logs/browser persistent storage.

On submit, atomic consume binds the authorization to one immutable intent. Monetary reservations are created at the dispatch gate, not by a dry-run preflight. Rechecking an authorization consumed by this same intent is allowed; another intent cannot reuse it. Client retries with same idempotency key return the same intent; token replay with another intent is rejected. A short permit performs final dispatch-time checks; if delay changes price above bound, passes next_review_at or crosses policy/window validity, stop for fresh authorization. Same immutable scope with a lower verified cost may proceed under a maximum-cost authorization if the policy explicitly permits it. Changed recipient/content/category always requires fresh scope.

Management approval and cost confirmation are independent. Approval is a policy-governed decision by qualified reviewers. Confirmation acknowledges a specific maximum financial exposure. Campaign authorization is run/snapshot-bound with a remaining total cap and exactly one child-intent membership per recipient. Machine consumers may use a reviewed API-key spending policy instead of human click confirmation only when org policy explicitly grants that mode; they cannot declare themselves exempt.

## Atomic reservation protocol

For an eligible intent, use doc 05's canonical protocol: a shared organization policy barrier, shared configuration/run locks and exclusive recipient/contact/conversation and monetary reservation locks. Ordinary sends do not update the organization row. Lock only applicable budget periods in sorted order. Load current permissions, suppression, frequency and approval authority. Compute verified conservative dispatch upper bound. All matching org/category/WABA/phone/campaign/principal budgets must have room for `posted + reserved + adjustments + new_reservation <= limit` where hard-stop applies. Equality is allowed; warning/approval thresholds evaluate the new projected amount.

Insert unique reservation and append ledger entry for each applicable budget and one frequency reservation. Check remaining run/approval cap in the same transaction. Consume the single-use authorization as applicable, write dispatch permit, audit evidence and job, then commit. No Redis counters or check-then-increment in separate transactions. Duplicate request cannot reserve twice. Every budget row has one currency; no implicit FX conversion.

Org-wide disable/policy/access topology publication takes the exclusive organization barrier; ordinary sends hold it shared. Suppression locks stable recipient/contact anchors, takeover locks the conversation, pause locks the run, and numeric budget/authority changes lock their own rows. Each writer conflicts with the corresponding dispatch lock through commit. Immediately before DISPATCHING, recheck current guards and reserved coverage and consume the permit. No human reply epoch is incremented. Changes after reservation cannot slip through by using an old permit; absence checks are protected by the anchor/topology protocol in doc 05. Rejected/revoked permits release only confirmed-undispatched reservations. Network requests happen outside the transaction. The precise guarantee is no new authorization after an earlier committed deny; a request authorized just before a deny may already be in flight. UI and audit preserve that boundary. A strict external account spending cap cannot be guaranteed by a local system when Meta billing arrives later or other senders exist.

## Settlement and reporting

| Observation | Money treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Definitely not dispatched / explicitly rejected before acceptance | Release reservation and frequency exposure with unique ledger entry |
| Accepted/sent, outcome pending | Keep reserved exposure; no invoice amount fabricated |
| Delivered with pricing category/billable metadata but no money | Store META_METADATA; derive cost only as INTERNAL_ESTIMATE using frozen applicable evidence; do not relabel it confirmed |
| Official invoice/statement with exact amount | Append reconciled amount/source; allocate per message only when evidence supports attribution |
| Unknown outcome or missing delivery status | Keep UNCERTAIN reservation until evidence resolves; missing webhook is not proof of zero charge |
| Corrected official evidence | Append reversal/correction referencing prior entry; retain original report basis |

Estimated exposure is the guard's accounting view; invoices are the financial source of truth where available. Display `estimated_amount`, `meta_billable/category/model`, `reconciled_amount`, `source`, `coverage`, `as_of` and variance separately. Do not sum duplicate multi-scope budget reservations as actual spend.

Freeze estimate/rate/policy/rounding snapshots for historical reports. Future rate publication never rewrites prior estimates. Actual delivery may occur after rate/period boundary: policy must determine the charge-effective instant; until verified, reserve worst-case covered rate across permitted delivery horizon or block near uncovered boundaries. At dispatch reallocate budget period for newly scheduled time; at reconciliation account for actual billed period with explicit adjustment, not silent history rewrite.

## Publication and operational controls

The Rate Card Registry follows **IMPORT/ENTER -> VALIDATE -> DIFF -> REVIEW -> PUBLISH**. There is no silent scraper or automatic publication. An operator uploads an official artifact or enters rows against an inspected official reference; manual entry does not lower the evidence requirement. Retain the source URL/title, retrieved_at, artifact/reference evidence and checksum, official provenance assessment, parser/import version, effective_from/effective_to with timezone basis, market, currency, category, billing unit, decimal rate, tier/rate-variant dimensions, reviewer, publication status and next_review_at. A reference without retrievable numerical evidence cannot establish a rate.

1. **IMPORT/ENTER:** create a DRAFT import and immutable source artifact, scoped to the organization. Record the current publication as base. Validate upload safety; never fetch an arbitrary supplied URL with privileged credentials.
2. **VALIDATE:** produce a versioned report checking provenance, typed decimal/currency values, billing units, complete required market/category/time coverage, dimension uniqueness, non-overlapping effective intervals and tiers, boundaries, rounding and sample calculations. Policy/discount predicates require their own reviewed evidence; a rate alone does not establish eligibility. Failure remains DRAFT with errors.
3. **DIFF:** after VALIDATED, compare against the base publication: additions/removals, changed amounts/rules/effective dates, coverage gaps and affected scheduled campaigns. DIFFED stores base ID, import revision and canonical report hash.
4. **REVIEW:** submit DIFFED as IN_REVIEW. A distinct authorized reviewer approves the exact import revision/source/validation/diff hashes as APPROVED or returns CHANGES_REQUIRED. Edits/resubmission return to DRAFT and invalidate prior reports/approval; no self-review. The review binds next_review_at and any accepted conservative upper-bound policy.
5. **PUBLISH:** an authorized publisher with recent MFA rechecks approval, current publisher and reviewer permission, hashes and coverage, then atomically creates an immutable publication/rate snapshot and selects it under the exclusive org policy barrier. The compare-and-set base must still equal the current head; otherwise STALE_RATE_BASE requires a new diff and review. Publication sets the import to PUBLISHED. Scheduling/dispatch select the reviewed effective interval within the current publication, never a draft.

Corrections create a new import and reviewed publication with supersedes_publication_id and correction rationale; never edit published amounts, evidence, dates or reviewer records, and never silently shorten an old row's effective_to. Each publication is a complete required coverage snapshot; old publications may overlap in time, but rows within one publication may not conflict. Existing estimates, reservations and invoice reconciliation retain their frozen publication/policy evidence. Queued dispatch reevaluates against the current head and its existing authorized maximum.

A review reminder does not certify rates: alert before next_review_at/coverage end; overdue review blocks new potentially paid dispatch until new reviewed evidence is published. Unknown future rates or missing required dimensions block affected schedules/dispatch. Source access failure never becomes validation success. Registry reports keep Meta-based estimates, internal reserved exposure and reconciled invoice amounts separate.

Budgets have frozen period boundaries/timezone, warning, hard stop, approval threshold and optional principal caps. Recommend organization total + marketing category + campaign caps initially; add per-phone/WABA only when operationally useful. Decreasing a limit below current exposure stops new sends without corrupting past reservations. Raising limits or overriding stops requires explicit permission, recent MFA and approval policy; changing role name has no effect.

If other apps/BSPs or the Business app can send on the same WABA, local guards cannot observe/reserve their spend before dispatch. Inventory this in connection setup. Label limits **WABA Control-controlled spend** until external usage is reliably imported; apply headroom or require exclusive gateway use. Coexistence stays disabled until this limitation is accepted. Alert on uncorrelated outgoing activity and reconciliation variance.

Race tests must include two senders taking the final budget unit, opt-out during campaign, expired quote in queue, changed template category, policy midnight, month boundary, duplicate settlement and uncertain-send recovery. See doc 17.
